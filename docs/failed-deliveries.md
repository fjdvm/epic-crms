# Failed delivery (#344)

`changeFailedDelivery(orderId, update, options)` requires the Fulfilment Module and a working shop Role on every call. All decisions wait for the storefront response. This backend slice adds no new screen; #345 owns the frontend. Supply a UUID `attemptId` once and keep it for explicit recovery after a timeout. CREOMS never automatically repeats an outside effect.

Updates are `record` (reason, Staff-selected `nextStep`: `rebook` or `request-cancellation`, and a Customer message), `rebook` (new Courier details), `restock`, or `write-off`. The Order stays `dispatched`. Cancellation is requested separately through the cancellation workflow; choosing `request-cancellation` here records intent and never cancels an Order or issues a refund.

Staff and Managers can record, rebook and restock. Write-off uses the shared Request approval workflow, valued from whole Order lines at selling price, excluding Delivery fee. One Manager-level approval is final at any amount (there is no CEO step). Admin can read history but cannot perform these shop actions. Approval is bound to the Order, attempt and selling amount. Changing the Admin setting changes subsequent requests.

The storefront retains `failed_deliveries` after rebooking, including reasons, Staff names, messages, dates, disposition and new Courier details. `loadFailedDeliveryHistory(orderId)` returns that Customer's history for Staff and the later COD flags work. A failure does not block COD. No Customer choice endpoint exists. Recording queues one generic App email and `delivery.failed` audit event under stable attempt keys. The existing durable stock outbox drains these without coupling email to audit availability. Rebooking queues `delivery.rebooked` with the executing Staff name and refreshes the Customer Order through its existing live event. Completed disposition and rebooking each queue a deduplicated Customer update, so later decisions do not leave the Customer with the original plan. Write-off requests emit `writeoff.requested`; execution emits `writeoff.approved`, alongside the approval workflow's named Manager decision records.

Restock uses the original immutable IMS Reservation/line keys. Its intent commits before IMS is called, so partial progress can only resume the same disposition. Rebooking is allowed only before stock disposition. Restock and write-off mark packed Reservation lines handled, preventing cancellation from adding written-off goods back. Packed goods were already deducted: write-off records the loss without deducting twice. Legacy Orders lacking IMS references refuse stock handling and ask Staff to reconcile. Disposed goods cannot be delivered or rebooked; a fresh Replacement Order belongs to the replacement flow.

Rollout: migrate the Ecommerce companion through `0032_failed_deliveries`, grant `SELECT` with `docs/db/creoms-failed-deliveries-readonly.sql`, and ship the storefront before CREOMS. No CREOMS schema change. Production migration and grant require the owner's approval for the exact commands. Live checks belong to a separate verification issue.

## Screen (#345)

`/orders/[id]/failed-delivery` (Fulfilment Module) records the reason and next step (re-deliver or cancel), sends the editable Customer message, then offers re-deliver (new Courier), back to stock and write-off. Reason and next-step choices are local and instant; saving, re-delivering, restocking and writing off wait for the server. Staff see "Request approval" for a write-off. The Figma "change to store pickup" option and the IMS batch and chiller-time card are not built: the storefront has no pickup switch and CREOMS has no IMS batch data yet. No schema change or migration.
