# Failed delivery (#344)

`recordFailedDelivery(orderId, update, options)` requires the Fulfilment Module and a working shop Role on every call. All decisions wait for the storefront response. This backend slice adds no new screen; #345 owns the frontend. Supply a UUID `attemptId` once and keep it for explicit recovery after a timeout. CREOMS never automatically repeats an outside effect.

Updates are `record` (reason, Staff-selected `nextStep`: `rebook` or `request-cancellation`, and a Customer message), `rebook` (new Courier details), `restock`, or `write-off`. The Order stays `dispatched`. Cancellation is requested separately through the cancellation workflow; choosing `request-cancellation` here records intent and never cancels an Order or issues a refund.

Staff and Managers can record, rebook and restock. Write-off uses the shared Request approval workflow, valued from whole Order lines at selling price, excluding Delivery fee. Exactly the configured Approval limit stops at the Manager; above it also needs the CEO. Admin and CEO can read history but cannot perform these shop actions. Approval is bound to the Order, attempt and selling amount. Changing the Admin setting changes subsequent requests.

The storefront retains `failed_deliveries` after rebooking, including reasons, Staff names, messages, dates, disposition and new Courier details. `loadFailedDeliveryHistory(orderId)` returns that Customer's history for Staff and the later COD flags work. A failure does not block COD. No Customer choice endpoint exists. Recording queues one generic App email and `delivery.failed` audit event under stable attempt keys. The existing durable stock outbox drains these without coupling email to audit availability. Write-off requests emit `writeoff.requested`; execution emits `writeoff.approved`, alongside the approval workflow's named Manager/CEO decision records.

Restock uses the original immutable IMS Reservation/line keys. Its intent commits before IMS is called, so partial progress can only resume the same disposition. Rebooking is allowed only before stock disposition. Restock and write-off mark packed Reservation lines handled, preventing cancellation from adding written-off goods back. Packed goods were already deducted: write-off records the loss without deducting twice. Legacy Orders lacking IMS references refuse stock handling and ask Staff to reconcile. Disposed goods cannot be delivered or rebooked; a fresh Replacement Order belongs to the replacement flow.
